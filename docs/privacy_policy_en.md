# Jaljarara Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-__-__ (set on store release)
Operator: (company or individual name) · Contact: (email)

Jaljarara ("the app") helps you keep track of your houseplants. The app works **without an account**. Apart from the cases described below, it does not send your information to any server or keep it anywhere other than your device.

## 1. Information stored only on your device

The following stays on your device. The operator cannot access it.

- Plants you add (name, species, last watered date, watering interval, memo)
- Plant diary entries (photos and notes)
- Plant photos (downsized copies of photos you take or pick)
- Reminder time, reminder pause and display settings
- Identification history (local path of the photos used and the candidate list)

Deleting the app deletes all of this information.

## 2. Information sent outside the app

### 2-1. Photo identification (Pl@ntNet)

Only when you use "Identify by photo", the photos you choose are resized to 1024 px or less, **stripped of metadata such as location (EXIF)**, and sent to the plant identification service Pl@ntNet (my-api.plantnet.org, France).

- Purpose: to receive likely species for the plant in the photo
- Data sent: the resized, metadata-free photos (up to 5 at a time)
- Kept by the operator: **no.** The operator has no server and never receives or sees your photos.
- Processing by the recipient: governed by the Pl@ntNet privacy policy (https://plantnet.org/privacy)
- If you add a plant by searching its name or typing it in, no photo is sent anywhere.

### 2-2. Backup (no server)

The app has no login and no operator server. You can move your records in two ways, and the operator can see neither.

- **Export and import a file**: In My › Backup, the app packs your records and photos into one zip file, and you save it wherever you choose (your own drive, a messenger, and so on).
- **Automatic device backup**: If backup is turned on in your Android settings, the operating system backs up the app's records (without photos) to your Google account and restores them on a new device. On iPhone, your iCloud device backup settings apply.

### 2-3. Species catalog photos

Catalog photos are loaded from Wikimedia Commons (upload.wikimedia.org). As with any web image, Wikimedia receives your device's IP address when a photo loads. No other information is sent.

## 3. Permissions

| Permission | Used for | When it is requested |
|---|---|---|
| Camera | Taking plant photos (identification, diary) | The first time you use the camera |
| Photos / media | Picking a plant photo from your library | The first time you open the library |
| Notifications | Watering day reminders | On the first-launch screen (you can decline) |

Declining a permission only limits that feature.

## 4. Information we do not collect

- Location, contacts, advertising identifiers
- Usage analytics or tracking (no analytics tools are used)
- Payment information (no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads)

## 5. Deleting your data

No records are stored on an operator server, so there is no account to delete. Deleting the app removes all records on the device. You can delete any exported backup file yourself.

## 6. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the change and its effective date on this page and in the store listing.
